# Kings of Glory

Kings of Glory is an isometric, multiplayer PvE strategy game that runs in the browser.

It combines:

- Factorio-style automation and production chains
- The Settlers-style settlement building and resource management
- Civilization-style exploration, expansion, and strategic progression

The goal is to bring these systems together in a shared online world where players build, automate, trade, and cooperate against environmental challenges and threats.
